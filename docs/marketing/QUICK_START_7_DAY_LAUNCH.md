## 🚨 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### DON'Ts ❌
- ❌ Post same content to multiple subreddits in one day (looks spammy)
- ❌ Ignore comments (respond within 1 hour)
- ❌ Only promote (add value first)
- ❌ Launch Product Hunt on Monday or Friday (worst days)
- ❌ Forget to track where signups come from
- ❌ Overpromise features that don't exist yet

### DO's ✅
- ✅ Be authentic and humble
- ✅ Share your journey (people love founder stories)
- ✅ Respond to every comment
- ✅ Ask for specific feedback
- ✅ Thank everyone profusely
- ✅ Ship updates weekly

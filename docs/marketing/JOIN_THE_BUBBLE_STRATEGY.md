# 🫧 "Join the Bubble" Marketing Strategy
## Making Events Discoverable & Social at Real-World Gatherings

**Campaign Tagline:** *"Your crew's having a party. Join the bubble."*

**Core Insight:** The best event marketing happens at other events. When people see others having fun with PartyHause games and experiences, they want in immediately.

---

## 🎯 The Use Case

**Scenario:**
> *You're at a concert, festival, or party. Your friends are using PartyHause to run games, polls, and activities. You want to join their "bubble" and participate in the fun without being a formal guest.*

**User Journey:**
1. **Discovery:** See friends playing a PartyHause game at an event
2. **Interest:** "What is that? Can I join?"
3. **Connection:** Friend adds you to their crew via QR code
4. **Access:** Their network events appear in your Explore feed
5. **Participation:** Join their activities, games, and experiences
6. **Retention:** Now you see all your crew's future events
7. **Conversion:** You create your own event with PartyHause

---

## 🔥 Viral Mechanics

### 1. **"Crew Streaks"**
- Track consecutive weeks attending crew events
- Badges for 5, 10, 20, 50 crew events
- Share streaks on social media
- Competition among friend groups

### 2. **"Crew Size Leaderboards"**
- Gamify crew building
- "Top Connectors" weekly rankings
- Unlock features at crew milestones
- Social status = large, active crew

### 3. **"Event Invites = App Invites"**
- Every event invitation is an app download opportunity
- "Join [Friend]'s crew to RSVP"
- Seamless onboarding through invitation link
- Viral loop built into core functionality

## 💡 Best Practices Summary

### Response Time Goals
```
Support Emails:     24-48 hours
Privacy Requests:   6 hours (acknowledgment), 30 days (completion)
Legal Inquiries:    24-48 hours
Bug Reports:        12-24 hours (acknowledgment)
Feature Requests:   48 hours
```

### Email Etiquette
1. ✅ Always personalize (use customer's name)
2. ✅ Be empathetic and understanding
3. ✅ Provide clear next steps
4. ✅ Include relevant links (support page, FAQs)
5. ✅ Use professional signature
6. ✅ Follow up to ensure resolution
7. ✅ Thank customers for their patience

### Privacy Email Protocol
1. 🔒 Respond within 6 hours (acknowledgment)
2. 🔒 Verify identity before processing
3. 🔒 Complete within 30 days (GDPR/CCPA requirement)
4. 🔒 Document all privacy requests
5. 🔒 Use reference numbers for tracking
6. 🔒 Secure communication only

## 🔧 Setup Options Comparison

| Feature | Free Forwarding | Google Workspace | Help Desk Platform |
|---------|----------------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Cost** | $0/month | $6/month | $15-49/month |

| **Scalability** | Low | Medium | High |
| **Team Collaboration** | Limited | Good | Excellent |
| **Ticketing System** | No | No | Yes |
| **Analytics** | No | Basic | Advanced |
| **Best For** | Solo/MVP | Small team | Growing team |

**Recommended Path**:
1. **Start**: Free forwarding (MVP launch)
2. **Upgrade**: Google Workspace (post-launch)
3. **Scale**: Help desk platform (when >50 emails/day)

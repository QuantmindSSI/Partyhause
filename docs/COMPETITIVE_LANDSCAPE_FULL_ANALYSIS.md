**Date**: November 4, 2025  
**Analysis**: 15 Event & Collaboration Apps  
**Category**: Event Management, Invitations, and Collaborative Planning

---

## 📋 Executive Summary

The event planning market is fragmented across multiple categories:
- **Professional Events**: Luma, Eventbrite, Hopin
- **Personal Invitations**: Evite, Punchbowl, Paperless Post
- **Emerging Players**: Partiful, Mixily, Hobnob
- **Social Platforms**: Facebook Events, Meetup
- **Enterprise Tools**: Cvent, Bizzabo, Splash

**PartyHause's Opportunity**: Create the first truly mobile-first, music-integrated, social platform specifically for personal celebrations with a focus on guest experience and real-time engagement.

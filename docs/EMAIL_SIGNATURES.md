## 🚀 How to Add Signature in Gmail

### Method 1: Web Interface
1. Open Gmail
2. Click Settings (gear icon) → See all settings
3. Scroll down to "Signature" section
4. Click "Create new" and give it a name (e.g., "PartyHause Support")
5. Paste your signature (use HTML for formatted version)
6. Configure options:
   - ✓ Insert this signature before quoted text in replies
   - Choose "For new emails use: [Your signature name]"
   - Choose "On reply/forward: [Your signature name]"
7. Scroll down and click "Save Changes"

### Method 2: Gmail Mobile App
1. Open Gmail app
2. Tap menu (≡) → Settings
3. Select your account
4. Tap "Mobile signature"
5. Enter your text signature (HTML not supported on mobile)
6. Tap OK

## 📝 Customization Tips

### Colors by Department:
- **Support**: Purple/Indigo (#6366F1)
- **Privacy**: Green (#10B981)
- **Legal**: Amber/Yellow (#F59E0B)
- **Executive**: Deep Purple (#8B5CF6)

### Font Recommendations:
- Primary: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont (system fonts)
- Fallback: 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
- Size: 13-14px for body, 16-18px for name

### Logo Requirements:
- Square logo: 60x60px or 80x80px
- Format: PNG with transparency
- Host on: https://www.partyhause.com/images/
- File: logo-square.png

### Headshot (for executives):
- Size: 80x80px or 100x100px
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Style: Professional, circular crop
- Background: Neutral or transparent

## 🎯 Signature Best Practices

### Do's:
✅ Keep it concise (4-6 lines max)
✅ Include only essential contact info
✅ Use professional colors matching brand
✅ Test on multiple email clients
✅ Include mobile-friendly version
✅ Link to support resources
✅ Update regularly (check links work)

### Don'ts:
❌ Don't use large images (slow load time)
❌ Don't include too many links
❌ Don't use animated GIFs
❌ Don't add quotes or personal messages
❌ Don't use bright/clashing colors
❌ Don't make it longer than 8 lines

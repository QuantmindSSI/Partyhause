# PartyHause Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

## Primary Target: The Bold Creator 🪄

### Demographics
- **Age**: 25-45 years old
- **Gender**: 60% female, 40% male
- **Location**: Urban markets (NYC, LA, SF, Austin, Miami, Chicago, Seattle)
- **Income**: $60,000-$150,000 annually
- **Occupation**: Creative professionals, designers, marketers, entrepreneurs, experience managers
- **Education**: College-educated, often in creative or business fields

### Psychographics
- **Values**: Innovation, creativity, self-expression, memorable experiences
- **Personality**: Extroverted, adventurous, trendsetting, detail-oriented
- **Lifestyle**: Active social life, early adopter of technology, Instagram/TikTok active
- **Aspirations**: Create "wow moments," be known for amazing events, build reputation as great host
- **Pain Points**: 
  - Existing tools are boring and transactional
  - Can't express creativity through standard platforms
  - Entertainment requires separate coordination
  - No way to showcase event creativity to others

### Behavioral Characteristics
- **Event Frequency**: 6-12 events per year (mix of personal and semi-professional)
- **Event Types**: 
  - Themed birthday parties (80s night, murder mystery, costume parties)
  - Launch parties for creative projects
  - Immersive dinner experiences
  - Art shows and gallery openings
  - Milestone celebrations with unique concepts
- **Event Budget**: $1,000-$5,000 per event
- **Tech Savviness**: High - early adopter of new apps and platforms
- **Social Media**: Very active on Instagram (visual), TikTok (trends), Pinterest (inspiration)
- **Current Tools**: Cobbling together Canva + Eventbrite + Pinterest + manual entertainment planning

### Goals and Motivations
- **Primary Goal**: Create unforgettable, Instagram-worthy experiences
- **Secondary Goals**: 
  - Build reputation as creative host
  - Bring people together through unique experiences
  - Express personal creativity and style
  - Create shareable moments
- **Motivations**: Recognition, self-expression, connection, joy

### PartyHause Value Proposition
- **30+ interactive games** enable unique entertainment without vendor coordination
- **AR treasure hunts** transform venues into immersive experiences
- **Professional invitation design studio** enables creative expression
- **AI highlight reels** create shareable content showcasing their creativity
- **Visual planning boards** organize creative concepts in one place
- **Memory timelines** build portfolio of amazing events hosted

### Acquisition Channels
1. **Instagram/TikTok ads**: Visual showcases of unique events
2. **Influencer partnerships**: Lifestyle and event creators
3. **Product Hunt**: Tech-savvy early adopters
4. **Pinterest**: Event planning inspiration seekers
5. **Word-of-mouth**: Guests at creative events become hosts

### Success Metrics
- **Activation**: Creates first event within 3 days
- **Engagement**: Uses 3+ interactive games per event
- **Retention**: Hosts 2nd event within 60 days
- **Advocacy**: Shares AI highlight reel on social media
- **Revenue**: Upgrades to premium within 2 events

## Secondary Target: The Mindful Host 💡

### Demographics
- **Age**: 28-50 years old
- **Gender**: 70% female, 30% male
- **Location**: Urban and suburban markets with wellness culture (Portland, Boulder, San Diego, Austin, Asheville)
- **Income**: $50,000-$120,000 annually
- **Occupation**: Wellness professionals, yoga instructors, therapists, educators, non-profit workers
- **Education**: College-educated, often in health, education, or social sciences

### Psychographics
- **Values**: Authenticity, sustainability, mindfulness, community, intentionality
- **Personality**: Introspective, empathetic, values-driven, thoughtful
- **Lifestyle**: Wellness-focused (yoga, meditation, organic food), conscious consumer
- **Aspirations**: Create meaningful connections, foster authentic community, live values
- **Pain Points**:
  - Existing platforms feel commercial and transactional
  - No tools aligned with wellness/sustainability values
  - Want meaningful engagement, not superficial entertainment
  - Desire intentional memory-keeping

### Behavioral Characteristics
- **Event Frequency**: 4-8 events per year
- **Event Types**:
  - Yoga retreats and wellness workshops
  - Mindful gatherings and meditation circles
  - Sustainable celebration dinners
  - Book clubs with intention-setting
  - Nature-based celebrations (solstice, equinox)
- **Event Budget**: $500-$2,500 per event
- **Tech Savviness**: Medium - selective about technology use
- **Social Media**: Moderate presence, prefers authentic content over polished posts
- **Current Tools**: Basic invitations (Evite) or manual email coordination

### Goals and Motivations
- **Primary Goal**: Foster authentic, meaningful connections
- **Secondary Goals**:
  - Create intentional, nourishing experiences
  - Build conscious community
  - Align celebrations with values
  - Preserve meaningful memories
- **Motivations**: Purpose, connection, authenticity, growth

### PartyHause Value Proposition
- **Intentional design** that feels authentic, not commercial
- **Connection-building features** (icebreaker games, meaningful prompts)
- **Memory preservation** that honors the significance of gatherings
- **Collaborative planning** enables shared intention-setting
- **Post-event gratitude** features align with mindfulness practices
- **Eco-friendly digital invitations** reduce paper waste

### Acquisition Channels
1. **Wellness influencer partnerships**: Yoga teachers, meditation guides
2. **Content marketing**: "Mindful event planning," "Creating intentional gatherings"
3. **Community partnerships**: Yoga studios, wellness centers, meditation apps
4. **Word-of-mouth**: Trusted recommendations within wellness communities
5. **LinkedIn**: Professional wellness practitioners

### Success Metrics
- **Activation**: Creates first event within 7 days
- **Engagement**: Uses icebreaker/connection games
- **Retention**: Hosts 2nd event within 90 days
- **Advocacy**: Recommends to wellness community
- **Revenue**: Subscribes for ongoing community features

## ICP Prioritization Matrix

### Phase 1: Launch (Months 0-6)
**Primary Focus**: Bold Creators
- **Rationale**: Underserved niche, vocal advocates, social media presence, willing to try new platforms
- **Target**: 1,000 active users
- **Geography**: NYC, LA, SF, Austin, Miami

### Phase 2: Early Growth (Months 6-12)
**Primary Focus**: Bold Creators (continued) + Mindful Hosts
- **Rationale**: Expand to second underserved archetype with strong community
- **Target**: 5,000 active users
- **Geography**: Add Portland, Boulder, San Diego, Seattle

### Phase 3: Expansion (Months 12-18)
**Primary Focus**: Intimate Curators
- **Rationale**: Larger market, upgrade from free tools, high event frequency
- **Target**: 20,000 active users
- **Geography**: Expand to top 20 US metro areas

### Phase 4: Scale (Months 18-24)
**Primary Focus**: All archetypes + Corporate/Culture Catalysts
- **Rationale**: Full market coverage, enterprise opportunities
- **Target**: 100,000 active users
- **Geography**: National + international expansion

## Segmentation Strategy

### Acquisition Segmentation
- **Innovators (2.5%)**: Bold Creators in tech hubs
- **Early Adopters (13.5%)**: Bold Creators + Mindful Hosts
- **Early Majority (34%)**: Intimate Curators
- **Late Majority (34%)**: All archetypes, mainstream adoption
- **Laggards (16%)**: Not target market

### Behavioral Segmentation
- **Power Hosts**: 12+ events/year, premium subscribers, advocates
- **Regular Hosts**: 4-11 events/year, mix of free and paid
- **Occasional Hosts**: 1-3 events/year, mostly free tier
- **Guests**: Invited to events, potential hosts

### Value-Based Segmentation
- **High LTV**: Power hosts, premium subscribers, frequent events
- **Medium LTV**: Regular hosts, occasional premium features
- **Low LTV**: Occasional hosts, free tier
- **Viral Value**: Guests who become hosts, social media advocates

### Feature-Based Segmentation
- **Entertainment Seekers**: Use interactive games heavily
- **Memory Makers**: Focus on AI highlight reels and photo organization
- **Planners**: Use collaborative boards and vendor coordination
- **Networkers**: Engage with discovery and community features

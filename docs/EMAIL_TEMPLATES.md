### 6. RSVP Not Working
```
Subject: Re: RSVP Issue

Hi [Name],

I'm sorry you're experiencing issues with RSVPs! Let's troubleshoot this together.

**Quick Checks:**

1. **Event Published?** 
   - Guests can only RSVP to published events
   - Check if your event status is "Published" (not "Draft")

2. **Invitations Sent?**
   - Verify invitations were sent from the guest list
   - Check the "Sent" status for each guest

3. **Email Delivery:**
   - Ask guests to check their spam/junk folders
   - Invitation emails come from noreply@partyhause.com

4. **RSVP Link:**
   - The RSVP link in the invitation should work
   - Try clicking it yourself to test

**What I Can Do:**

Reply with your event details and I'll:
- Check invitation delivery status
- Resend invitations if needed
- Verify RSVP links are working
- Manually mark RSVPs if needed

You can also view RSVP status anytime by going to your event → Guests tab.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
PartyHause Support Team

---
💬 We're here to help!
```

## 📋 Gmail Canned Response Setup

To use these templates in Gmail:

1. **Enable Canned Responses:**
   - Gmail Settings > Advanced
   - Enable "Templates"
   - Save Changes

2. **Create Templates:**
   - Compose new email
   - Paste template text
   - Click three dots (⋮) > Templates > Save draft as template
   - Name it (e.g., "Support - Bug Report")

3. **Use Templates:**
   - When replying to email
   - Click three dots (⋮) > Templates
   - Select your template
   - Customize with specific details
   - Send!

## 💡 Best Practices

1. **Personalize Every Response**
   - Use customer's name
   - Reference specific details from their email
   - Don't sound robotic

2. **Be Empathetic**
   - Acknowledge frustration
   - Apologize when appropriate
   - Show you care about their experience

3. **Be Clear and Concise**
   - Answer their question directly
   - Use bullet points
   - Avoid jargon

4. **Provide Next Steps**
   - Tell them what to expect
   - Give them actions to take
   - Offer further help

5. **Follow Up**
   - Check if issue is resolved
   - Ask for feedback
   - Thank them for patience

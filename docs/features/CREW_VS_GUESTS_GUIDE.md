# PartyCrew vs Guests: Complete Differentiation Guide
**Created:** November 1, 2025  
**Status:** Brand Definition  
**Priority:** Critical - Core Concept

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

**PartyHaus has TWO distinct relationship types:**

1. **PartyCrew** = Your social network (followers/following on the platform)
2. **Guests** = People invited to/attending specific events

**Key Insight:** Someone can be in your PartyCrew WITHOUT being a guest at your event, and vice versa. These are independent, complementary systems.

---

## 📚 Clear Definitions

### PartyCrew (Social Network Layer)

**Definition:** PartyCrew members are people who **follow you on the PartyHaus platform** to stay updated about your events and activity. This is a **persistent, ongoing relationship** similar to Instagram followers or LinkedIn connections.

**Characteristics:**
- ✅ Platform-level relationship (not event-specific)
- ✅ Persistent across all events you create
- ✅ Opt-in by the follower (they choose to join your crew)
- ✅ Visible in your profile stats
- ✅ Receive notifications when you create new events
- ✅ See your events in their social feed
- ✅ No limit on crew size (can have thousands)
- ✅ Public or private (you control who can join)

**Where They Appear:**
- Your profile page under "PartyCrew" count
- "My PartyCrew" list (people who follow you)
- "Crewing With" list (people you follow)
- Discover/search pages
- Social feed algorithms

---

### Guests (Event Attendance Layer)

**Definition:** Guests are people **invited to or attending a specific event**. This is a **temporary, event-specific relationship** that exists only for that particular occasion.

**Characteristics:**
- ✅ Event-specific relationship (tied to one event only)
- ✅ Temporary (ends when event ends)
- ✅ Requires invitation OR discovery of public event
- ✅ Visible in event attendee list
- ✅ Has RSVP status (Going, Maybe, Not Going)
- ✅ Can attend event even if not in your PartyCrew
- ✅ Limited by event capacity/privacy settings
- ✅ May or may not be platform users (can invite via email/SMS)

**Where They Appear:**
- Event details page under "Guests" or "Attendees"
- Event RSVP list
- Event check-in system
- Event-specific notifications
- Guest management dashboard

## 🎬 Real-World Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Wedding Planner (Sarah)

**Sarah's PartyCrew:**
- 2,500 followers (couples getting married, wedding vendors, event lovers)
- Built over 2 years of hosting events
- People follow her for inspiration and to discover her new events

**Sarah's Pool Party Event Guests:**
- 50 invited guests (her close friends and family)
- Only 12 of the 50 guests are in her PartyCrew
- The other 38 guests don't follow her on PartyHaus (they're personal friends)
- Her 2,488 other PartyCrew members can **see** the event in their feed but aren't **invited**

**Key Insight:** Sarah has a large professional following (PartyCrew), but her personal pool party is intimate with mostly non-followers (regular guests).

---

### Scenario 2: DJ (Marcus)

**Marcus's PartyCrew:**
- 10,000 followers (fans, club-goers, music lovers)
- They follow him to find out where he's performing next
- Mostly young adults interested in nightlife

**Marcus's Club Night Event Guests:**
- 200 ticket holders (people actually attending)
- 150 of the 200 are from his PartyCrew (they discovered it through his profile/feed)
- 50 are walk-ins or found out through other means
- His remaining 9,850 PartyCrew members see the event but aren't attending this specific night

**Key Insight:** High conversion from PartyCrew to Guests. His social network directly drives event attendance.

---

### Scenario 3: First-Time Host (Alex)

**Alex's PartyCrew:**
- 0 followers (brand new to PartyHaus)
- Just signed up to plan his birthday party

**Alex's Birthday Party Guests:**
- 30 invited friends (from his phone contacts)
- 0 of them are on PartyHaus yet
- Alex sends SMS/email invites to bring them to the platform
- **Conversion Opportunity:** These 30 guests might join PartyHaus and become his first PartyCrew members!

**Key Insight:** You don't need PartyCrew to host events. Guests are the starting point, PartyCrew grows over time.

---

### Scenario 4: Community Organizer (Priya)

**Priya's PartyCrew:**
- 500 followers (local community members, neighbors, activists)
- They follow her for community events and updates

**Priya's Neighborhood Clean-Up Event:**
- 80 volunteers registered (guests)
- 60 are from her PartyCrew (they saw it in their feed)
- 20 are new volunteers who found it via Facebook share
- **Bonus:** Those 20 new volunteers see "Join PartyCrew" prompt during RSVP

**Key Insight:** PartyCrew acts as a distribution channel. Events get promoted to your network automatically.

---

### Scenario 5: Corporate Event Planner (James)

**James's PartyCrew:**
- 5,000 followers (HR professionals, corporate event planners, vendors)
- Professional network built through industry events

**James's Client's Conference Event:**
- 300 registered attendees (guests)
- 0 are from his PartyCrew (they're the client's employees)
- James's PartyCrew can see he's hosting it (social proof of his work)
- Privacy setting: "Crew can see event exists but can't RSVP (private event)"

**Key Insight:** PartyCrew serves as portfolio/social proof even for private client events.

## 🔧 Technical Implementation Differences

### Database Structure

```sql
-- PartyCrew = Social Network (Persistent)
CREATE TABLE connections (
  follower_id UUID,      -- Person following
  following_id UUID,     -- Person being followed
  created_at TIMESTAMPTZ -- When they joined crew
);

-- Guests = Event Attendance (Temporary)
CREATE TABLE event_attendees (
  event_id UUID,         -- Specific event
  user_id UUID,          -- Person attending
  rsvp_status TEXT,      -- Going/Maybe/No
  attended BOOLEAN       -- Did they show up?
);
```

### API Endpoints

```typescript
// PartyCrew APIs (Social Network)
GET /api/users/[id]/crew          // Get someone's followers
GET /api/users/[id]/crewing-with  // Get who they follow
POST /api/connections/join        // Follow someone
POST /api/connections/leave       // Unfollow someone

// Guests APIs (Event Attendance)
GET /api/events/[id]/guests       // Get event attendees
POST /api/events/[id]/invite      // Invite someone to event
POST /api/events/[id]/rsvp        // RSVP to event
POST /api/events/[id]/check-in    // Check-in guest at event
```

### Data Queries

```typescript
// Find: "Events from my PartyCrew"
SELECT e.* FROM events e
JOIN connections c ON e.organizer_id = c.following_id
WHERE c.follower_id = current_user_id;

// Find: "My PartyCrew members attending this event"
SELECT u.* FROM users u
JOIN connections c ON u.id = c.following_id
JOIN event_attendees ea ON u.id = ea.user_id
WHERE c.follower_id = current_user_id 
  AND ea.event_id = current_event_id
  AND ea.rsvp_status = 'going';

// Find: "Events I'm attending"
SELECT e.* FROM events e
JOIN event_attendees ea ON e.id = ea.event_id
WHERE ea.user_id = current_user_id
  AND ea.rsvp_status = 'going';
```

## 📊 User Journey Examples

```
1. Alex gets invited to Sarah's pool party (becomes GUEST)

3. Alex sees "Join Sarah's PartyCrew to see future events" prompt
4. Alex clicks "Join PartyCrew" (becomes CREW MEMBER + GUEST)
```

## 🎯 Key Business Metrics

### PartyCrew Metrics (Social Network Health)

```
- Total PartyCrew Size: How many followers you have
- PartyCrew Growth Rate: New followers per week
- PartyCrew Engagement: % who interact with your content
- Crew Retention: % still following after 6 months
- Mutual Crew: How many follow you back
- Crew Demographics: Age, location, interests
- Feed Reach: How many crew see your posts
```

### Guest Metrics (Event Performance)

```
- Guest Count: Attendees per event
- RSVP Rate: % invited who respond
- Attendance Rate: % who actually show up
- Guest Satisfaction: Post-event ratings
- Guest Conversion: % guests who join PartyCrew
- Guest Retention: % who attend multiple events
- Capacity Utilization: Guests / max capacity
```

### Cross-Metric (Crew→Guest Conversion)

```
- Crew-to-Guest Rate: % of your crew who attend events
- Discovery Rate: % of guests who found event via crew feed
- Social Proof Impact: Attendance lift from "X crew attending"
- Crew Guest Value: Revenue from crew vs non-crew guests
- Viral Coefficient: New crew gained per event hosted
```

## 💡 Product Strategy Insights

### Why This Separation Matters

1. **Different Lifecycles**
   - PartyCrew = Long-term, persistent (years)
   - Guests = Short-term, temporary (hours/days)

2. **Different Purposes**
   - PartyCrew = Discovery, distribution, community
   - Guests = Logistics, attendance, coordination

3. **Different Privacy Needs**
   - PartyCrew = Public or private follower lists
   - Guests = Per-event privacy (hide attendee list)

4. **Different Notifications**
   - PartyCrew = "New event posted", "Creator update"
   - Guests = "Event reminder", "Check-in time", "Location change"

5. **Different Growth Strategies**
   - PartyCrew = Content, consistency, engagement
   - Guests = Invitations, word-of-mouth, capacity

## 🎪 Marketing Language Guide

### ❌ DON'T Say:
- "Add to guest list" (when you mean follow)
- "Followers" (too generic, use PartyCrew)
- "Friends" (too Facebook, unclear)
- "Invite to profile" (doesn't make sense)
- "Subscribe" (too newsletter-y)

### ✅ DO Say:

**For PartyCrew (Social Network):**
- "Join [Name]'s PartyCrew"
- "Start crewing with [Name]"
- "Become a crew member"
- "Build your PartyCrew"
- "See events from your crew"
- "X people in your PartyCrew"

**For Guests (Event Attendance):**
- "Add to guest list"
- "Invite guests"
- "RSVP as guest"
- "X guests attending"
- "Manage your guests"
- "Guest check-in"

**For Overlap (Crew + Guests):**
- "5 people in your PartyCrew are attending"
- "Crew members going to this event"
- "Your crew is interested in this"
- "Invite your PartyCrew"

| Feature | PartyCrew (Social Network) | Guests (Event Attendance) |
|---------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Relationship Type** | Persistent, ongoing | Temporary, event-specific |
| **Scope** | Platform-wide | Single event only |

| **Visibility** | Profile stats, crew lists | Event attendee list |

| **Privacy Control** | Public/private profile | Per-event guest list privacy |
| **Limit** | Unlimited followers | Event capacity limit |
| **Platform Requirement** | Must have PartyHaus account | Can be off-platform (email) |
| **Purpose** | Discovery & distribution | Logistics & coordination |
| **Example Count** | "2.5K PartyCrew" | "50 Guests" |
| **Growth Strategy** | Content & engagement | Invitations & sharing |
| **Metric Focus** | Follower growth, engagement | Attendance rate, RSVP |

## ✅ Clarity Checklist

When implementing any feature, ask:

- [ ] Is this about **social relationships** (PartyCrew) or **event attendance** (Guests)?
- [ ] Does the copy clearly use "PartyCrew" vs "Guests"?
- [ ] Are the icons distinct? (👥 for crew, 🎫 for guests)
- [ ] Can a user be one without the other?
- [ ] Does the UI show both counts separately?
- [ ] Are notifications labeled correctly?
- [ ] Does the privacy setting apply to the right entity?

---

**Bottom Line:** 
- **PartyCrew** = Who follows you (Instagram-like)
- **Guests** = Who's invited/attending an event (Eventbrite-like)
- **Magic happens** when your PartyCrew discovers your events and becomes Guests! 🎉

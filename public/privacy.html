Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-06. Effective September 6, 2026.

PartyHause is an iPhone app for one host to manage private events, manually enter up to 50 guests, send invitations, receive browser RSVPs, and record attendance. We collect only the information needed to provide and secure those functions.

Information We Process

Host account

We process your name, normalized email address, password hash, email-verification state, account dates, and the versions of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted. Passwords are hashed and are not stored as readable text.

Private events and guests

We process event name, date, time, timezone, location, description, lifecycle state, and the guest names and email addresses a host enters. We also process invitation-delivery state, RSVP choice and response time, and manual check-in state. Guests use a scoped browser link and do not need a PartyHause account.

Operations and support

We process bounded service and security records such as request time, route, status, and infrastructure network information. If you email support, we process the message and address needed to answer it. PartyHause does not use advertising identifiers or cross-company tracking.

How We Use Information

  • Create, verify, secure, and recover host accounts.
  • Store private events and guest lists selected by the host.
  • Send fixed invitation and account emails and record accurate delivery outcomes.
  • Resolve browser RSVPs and show the host current RSVP and attendance state.
  • Prevent abuse, investigate failures, and meet legal obligations.

Processors

  • Microsoft Azure: application hosting, PostgreSQL database, blob storage, and Azure Communication Services email delivery.
  • Resend: fallback transactional email delivery when configured or when the primary email provider fails.
  • Expo: iOS build processing used to produce release binaries.
  • Apple: App Store distribution and Apple-controlled purchase-free download records.

Processors may retain their own security and delivery records under their published terms where PartyHause cannot shorten that period. A delivered email is a copy in the recipient's mailbox and cannot be recalled when a guest or host account is deleted.

No Sale, Ads, Tracking, or Payments

PartyHause does not sell personal information, serve advertising, track people across other companies' apps or websites, or process payments in this release. The iPhone app requests no sensitive device permissions.

Account Deletion and Guest Data

A signed-in host can choose Account, then Delete Account. Password reauthentication and the exact confirmation word are required. Confirmation revokes all sessions and invitation links immediately. Primary database records and proven account-owned Azure blobs are then erased, normally in the same request and no later than 24 hours.

If another host independently entered you as a guest for that host's event, deleting your PartyHause account removes the account link but does not delete the other host's guest record. That record follows the event's 30-day guest retention rule. Contact the host or support@partyhause.com if it is inaccurate.

Your Choices

You can request access, correction, or help with a privacy concern by emailing support@partyhause.com. Account deletion is available directly in the app and does not require contacting support. We may retain narrowly scoped information when law requires a legal hold, and we restrict and audit that exception.

Age Eligibility

Host accounts are for people who are at least 18 years old. Signup requires an age-eligibility assertion.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, a new version will be published and new account consent will identify that version. The version above is the version accepted by current signup.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create my first event?
Open the app, tap the "+" button, choose an event template (like Birthday or Wedding), fill in the details, add guests, and publish! You can start with one of our templates or create a custom event.
How do I invite guests to my event?
When creating an event, tap "Add Guests" and select contacts from your phone or manually enter email addresses. PartyHause will send beautiful email invitations to your guests with RSVP options.
Can I track who has RSVP'd?
Yes! Go to your event, tap "Guests," and you'll see real-time RSVP status for all invitees. You can also check who has viewed the invitation and send reminders to those who haven't responded.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely! We use industry-standard encryption for all data in transit and at rest. We never sell your data, and you can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
How do I delete my account?
To delete your account and all your data, go to Settings > Account > Delete Account, or email us at privacy@partyhause.com with your request. We'll process deletions within 30 days.
Can I use PartyHause offline?
Some features work offline, like viewing events you've already loaded. However, creating events, sending invitations, and syncing guest RSVPs require an internet connection.
What event types does PartyHause support?
We support many event types including birthdays (adult & kids), weddings, baby showers, corporate events, product launches, fundraisers, and more! Each template includes customized features for that event type.
Is PartyHause free?
Yes! PartyHause is currently free to use with all features available. We may introduce premium features in the future, but core functionality will always remain free.
Can guests see other guests' contact information?
No. Guest privacy is important to us. Guests can only see the names of other attendees, not their email addresses or phone numbers, unless you explicitly share that information.
How do I report a bug or request a feature?
We'd love to hear from you! Email us at support@partyhause.com with bug reports or feature requests. Your feedback helps us improve!

🛠️ Troubleshooting

The app won't load or keeps crashing
Try these steps:
  1. Close and restart the app
  2. Check your internet connection
  3. Update to the latest version in the App Store
  4. Restart your device
  5. If the issue persists, contact support with your device model and iOS/Android version
I can't access my contacts
PartyHause needs permission to access your contacts. Go to your device Settings > PartyHause > enable Contacts permission. Then restart the app.
Invitations aren't being sent
Check that:
  • Email addresses are entered correctly
  • You have an active internet connection
  • You've published the event (not just saved as draft)
If issues persist, contact support and we'll investigate.
